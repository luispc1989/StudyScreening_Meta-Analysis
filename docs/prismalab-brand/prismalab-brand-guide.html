01 — Brand concept

From evidence to conclusion

PrismaLab is a platform for the full cycle of evidence synthesis — from protocol and literature search to systematic screening, data extraction, quality assessment and statistical meta-analysis. The name brings together PRISMA, the gold-standard reporting guideline for systematic reviews, and Lab, a space where rigorous, reproducible work happens.

The visual mark is a prism refracting a single beam of raw evidence into its structured components. The five coloured rays represent the phases of synthesis: search, screen, extract, appraise, and analyse. Where they converge is the conclusion — the pooled estimate that only a rigorous review can produce.

02 — The logo

Horizontal lockup

The primary logo is always used in its horizontal form. Three approved variants exist — one for each background tier. Never mix components from different variants.

Light — #FFFFFF

Mid-tone — #1C3558

Dark — #111111

Clear space

Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "P" in Prisma. No other visual elements should enter this zone.

06 — Usage rules

Do's and don'ts

The logo must always be reproduced from the official files. These rules protect consistency across all applications.

Do

Use the approved logo on approved backgrounds at the correct proportions.

Don't

Don't stretch or distort the logo. Always scale proportionally.

Don't

Don't change the colour of "Lab". It must always be #5DCAA5.

Don't

Don't place the logo on coloured or unapproved backgrounds. Only use the three approved background tiers.

Don't

Don't apply shadows, glows, or any visual effects to the logo.

Don't

Don't rotate or tilt the logo. It is always displayed horizontally.

Don't

Don't reduce to a single colour or use an outline/ghost version. The colour spectrum is essential.

Do
Use icon mark at small sizes or when context already establishes the PrismaLab name.

Use the icon mark for app icons, favicons, avatars, and UI elements below 240px.
